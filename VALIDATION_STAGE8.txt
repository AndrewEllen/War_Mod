Static validation performed:
- Active WarheadWorldRenderer does not submit VoxelImpactCloudRenderer.renderFire or renderSmoke.
- New packed particle engines do not call ParticleTypes or addParticle.
- Surface shockfront puffs and debris trail rendering are submitted by WarheadWorldRenderer.
- Temperature state drives fire-to-smoke colour/lifecycle changes.
- Nuclear particles circulate through centre rise, cap expansion, outer curl, under-cap return, and stem re-entry.
- Client command registration exposes packed/legacy renderer profiling modes and status counters.
- Sodium alone no longer forces the compatibility pipeline; the fallback is selected for an active Iris shader pack.
- Conventional hot fire is capped at 4.75 blocks above the impact surface.
- Terrain shockfront construction retains its final resolution and is amortised at 2,048 new samples per impact call.
- Debris swept-collision resolution and sample count are unchanged; repeated terrain lookups are cached per simulation step.
- Debris trail length and positions are unchanged; immutable trail snapshots are reused until the piece advances.
- Packed and legacy conventional profiling modes use the same soft particle mask.

Not performed in this environment:
- Full Gradle build.
- In-game runtime test.
- Vanilla/Sodium/Iris/shader-pack compatibility test.
- Frame-time or GPU-profiler capture.
